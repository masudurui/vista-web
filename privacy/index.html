Privacy Policy for Vista

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Vista is a local file browser for Mac. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Vista handles your data.

What Vista does with your files

Vista runs entirely on your Mac. Your files, folders, and search activity never leave your device. We do not upload, store, or access your files on any server.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not track how you use the app.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not send your files, search queries, or file names anywhere.

Visual Search model

If you enable Visual Search, Vista downloads a small on-device model (about 110 MB) the first time you use it. This model is used only on your Mac to find photos and videos by description. After the download, Visual Search works fully offline. No files or search queries are sent during or after the download.

Website

The Vista website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. We do not collect personal data through the website.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at support@vistaformac.com.
