Frequently asked questions

Does Vista slow down my Mac? ▼

No. Vista runs entirely on your Mac with optimized indexing that works in the background. It's designed to be lightweight and won't impact your system performance.

Can I undo actions? ▼

Yes. Every action in Vista is reversible. Files go to Trash first, and you can undo deletions with one click before permanently removing them.

How much storage can I reclaim? ▼

It depends on your file patterns. Users typically reclaim 10-50GB by removing duplicates, old archives, and large unused files. Vista shows you exactly what you can free up.

Which folders can Vista access? ▼

You control which folders Vista scans. By default, it organizes your Desktop, Downloads, and Documents. You can add or remove folders anytime in settings.

Is there a Pro version? ▼

Yes. Vista includes a 7-day free trial covering all features. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks Duplicates, Cleanup suggestions, and advanced search permanently.

Is my data really private? ▼

Absolutely. Vista has no servers, no accounts, and no cloud sync. All indexing and file processing happens locally on your Mac. We never see your files.
