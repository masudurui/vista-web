Frequently asked questions

How customizable is the interface? ▼

Completely. Colors, layouts, typography, icon styles—design it your way. Vista adapts to your aesthetic, not the other way around. No preset constraints.

Does Vista slow down my Mac? ▼

No. Optimized indexing runs in the background with minimal overhead. Vista is built for performance without sacrificing design.

Can I work with large file libraries? ▼

Yes. Vista scales beautifully to thousands of files. Fast search, instant thumbnails, and responsive UI even with massive libraries. Perfect for photographers, video editors, and creators.

Can I undo anything? ▼

Yes. Every destructive action is reversible. Files go to Trash first—undo with one click anytime, even after closing Vista.

How do I control my data? ▼

Complete control. Choose which folders Vista scans. No cloud, no accounts, no tracking. All processing happens on your Mac—your data, your rules.
