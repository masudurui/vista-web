Release notes

What’s new in Vista

Visual Search that runs on your Mac, text search inside more kinds of files, a calmer Clean Up, and a Home screen you can arrange. Here is everything in the latest release.

Version 1.0Build 2

13 November 2026 · macOS 14 or later · 7-day free trial

Download Vista Pricing

New in this release

Search by sight

Describe a photo or video and Vista finds it. Visual Search runs on your Mac with a one-time model download, then works offline. Videos are checked using a few frames from each.

Search inside more files

Vista can now read words in images, PDFs, and documents — including plain text, Markdown, RTF, Word, and Pages. Results show the matching line and highlight it in the file. PDF matches jump to the right page. Vista also spots links, emails, phone numbers, dates, amounts, and codes.

Smarter search

Results start with a Best Match view and show snippets explaining why each file matched. Sort by relevance, date, name, size, or kind. Move through suggestions with the arrow keys, then press Return or Esc.

A calmer Clean Up

Suggestions are split into “Safe to clear” and “Your call,” with a plain reason and the space each will free. Preview files before they move, switch between grid and list, and review exact copies or look-alike photos. Nothing is deleted — files go to the Trash.

Make Home yours

Reorder folders, hide the ones you don’t need, and change a folder’s name, color, shape, or icon. Apply one folder’s style to all folders, with matching or varied colors. Undo anytime or restore the defaults.

Move faster

The sidebar now has a 3×3 grid for your most-used destinations, with arrow-key navigation. Open Vista from the menu bar, press g then a letter to jump to a section, / to search, or ? for the shortcut list. Quick Settings gives you fast access to theme, text search, and Visual Search.

Better names

For screenshots, photos, and PDFs, “Suggest a Name” builds a clearer name from the text inside, plus its date or source. Review and edit the suggestions before renaming, and undo the batch later.

Clearer folder access

If macOS blocks a folder, Vista explains what happened, links to Privacy & Security settings, and lets you try again.

A simpler look

Sage is the new default. The theme picker is now Sage, White, and Dark. Dark has a softer charcoal background and off-white text.

Fixes

  • TypeScript files are no longer mistaken for videos
  • Folder watching no longer re-indexes dependency or build folders
  • Deleted or moved files drop out of search more reliably
  • PDFs with little or no readable text are retried instead of skipped permanently
  • Undoing a move from the Trash no longer creates duplicate entries
  • Search handles accents and punctuation more naturally
